## 0. Why these two, and what was weighed
The S11a1 cut list (its section 12) is five sessions of work; tonight has two Build stages. Chosen:
- **Friendly fire (Q22, decided yes; D 6.3).** Smallest sim change of the list (two owner tests in `combat.rs`), closes the "walk through your own
  barrage" exploit (S11a1 risk 4), and it is the one item that changes every mortar mission, so it gets the slice's balance reading (`ff_off`).
- **Counter-battery by sound (D 4.3) with AI counter-fire.** S11a1 leaves a gun in base with no answer (its risk 2). Displacement alone answers
  nothing (nobody hunts the gun yet); a marker plus counter-fire is the answer, displacement is the reply to it (later).
- **Crates instead of base rearm: NOT chosen (S11a2-8).** Read in the code: a served gun outside its base is already fed by a shells crate, HQ, dump or
  truck (`structures.rs` `rearm_forward`, S9); only the own-base skip is missing. AI guns never leave the base in S11a1, so crate-only shells would be
  bearers carrying a crate 20 m inside the base: the price is the same 1 MP per kg, the brake is small, and one 32 kg crate holds one 19 kg M1 round
  (5 Igr 18), a US penalty with no design intent. It belongs with tows and siting outside the base, where delivery is the cost (D 4.11).
Honest expectation: counter-fire is map fire (6 %, floor 50 m) on a marker with 5-15 % error, so few bursts land on the crew (D 4.3: "needs several
rounds"). The slice builds the mechanism and measures it (T141, T148); it does not promise that base guns die.

  marked: bool }`.

  `cb_stale_holds` (no new evidence since the last order), `cb_masked` (the sim would refuse: no order sent).
- Load validation (`load_rejects_bad_s11a2_state`, one case per check): `cb.len() <= 8`; `of` a player and not the holder; `pos`, `at` on the map;
  `err_mm <= map size`; `evidence >= 1`; `decay_at` in `(tick, tick + cb_decay_s]`; `mark_at` 0 or in `(tick, tick + cb_delay_s]` and then not

**Friendly fire, sim (Build A)**

  flight still passes through own men and own boxes (`first_box`, `first_static`, the capsule test at `combat.rs` 2105): only the burst is new.
  Where: `combat.rs` `Burst` += `ff: bool`, set where the shell burst is built (about 3207; `false` in the cook-off, item, charge and mine bursts).
  Keys `friendly_fire`. Off: 0. Test `direct_he_still_spares_own_men` (a tank HE burst and a grenade among own men with the switch 1: no own man
  hurt; control: the same burst from a mission round hurts). Save none (`Burst` is not saved).
- **F2 Blast and suppression.** In `MatchState::burst` (2375) the owner skip becomes `owner == b.player && !b.ff`: an own man takes suppression and,
  with a clear line, the overpressure damage as an enemy would. Overhead cover for an own victim: `piece_between` is asked with no excluded player
  (today it excludes the burst owner's pieces, so an own man under his own roof would have no roof). Own vehicles, statics, wire and tracks keep
  their owner tests (`first_box` 1993, statics 2428 and 2642, `blast_wire`, `blast_tracks`): S11a2-2. Test `own_mission_burst_hurts_own_men`
  (8 bombs on an own squad in the open: men lost; control `friendly_fire` 0: none, and hashes equal `<BASE>`'s for the same scene),
  `own_roof_shields_own_men_from_own_burst` (control: no roof).
- **F3 Fragments.** In `MatchState::fragments` (2594) the same test: own men join `men`; the "burst is on him" rule holds. Own vehicle frames and
  statics are not struck. Test `own_mission_fragments_hit_own_men` (own squad 8 m from the burst, outside the blast radius: wounds; control switch 0),
  `own_trench_shields_own_men_from_own_fragments` (the earth stops the rays as for an enemy).
- **F4 Credit and counters.** An own man killed by an own mission burst is no kill: not in `kills`, `kills_by`, `s7.kills_by_vehicle`, `gun_kills`,
  `note_gun_kill` (so T8's HE share and T135 keep their meaning); he counts in `ff_kills[0|1]` (mortar | gun, from the firing squad) and in his

**Counter-battery by sound, sim (Build A)**
- **K1 Evidence.** With `counter_battery` 1, each fire-mission round of an `inf_gun` (at the `Fired` point of `gunnery.rs` `gun_mission_tick`, where
  `gun_rounds` counts, 1133) is heard by every other player with a living soldier, ascending player id. For hearer `e`: the spot of `e.cb` with the
  same `of` whose `pos` is within `max(cb_reset_m, err_mm)` (the error only once marked) of the gun, nearest first, ties to the lower index, gets
  `evidence += 1`, `shots += 1`; none: a new spot (`evidence` 1, `decay_at = tick + cb_decay_s`); list full: the spot with the least evidence (ties:
  the oldest) is dropped first (`cb_dropped`). The firer's `cb_heard += 1`. Direct rounds and mortars leave no evidence (section 1). Where: new
  `crates/sim/src/counter_battery.rs` (`cb_note`, `cb_tick`, `CbSpot`, `MAX_CB_SPOTS`, validation); one call in `gunnery.rs`. Off: 0 (no spot is ever
  made). Test `gun_rounds_leave_evidence_with_the_enemy` (control `counter_battery` 0: `cb` empty; control: a mortar mission: empty),
  `a_short_hop_keeps_the_spot` (60 m: same spot; 80 m unmarked: a new one; 80 m with a marker error above 80 m: the same; D 10 test 5).
- **K2 Marker.** When a spot's evidence reaches `cb_rounds_to_mark` and it is not marked, `mark_at = tick + cb_delay_s`. `cb_tick` (called beside
  `update_intel`, `world.rs` 1559; works on ticks divisible by the tick rate and only with spots) makes the marker at `mark_at`: listener distance `L`
  = from `pos` to the hearer's nearest living soldier (ties: lower id; none alive: `mark_at` moves one second on); `err_mm = L * cb_error_pct / 100`;
  `at = pos + offset`, the offset drawn as the mission aim offset is (`fire_mission.rs` module docs: bearing uniform, length uniform to the error)
  from `RngStream::new(seed, tick, SimId(hearer << 8 | spot index), Purpose::CB_MARK)` (new purpose 39; the index is state, so the draw is the same
  on every machine), clamped to the map. `cb_marks += 1`, `cb_err_first_mm += err_mm`. Test
  `third_round_marks_the_gun_within_15_pct` (8 rounds, listener 800 m: no marker before round 3 + 15 s, then `|at - gun| <= 120 m`; control
  `cb_rounds_to_mark` 9: none), `slow_gun_is_marked_by_its_fourth_round` (one round a minute; control `cb_decay_s` 30: never).
- **K3 Sharpen, decay.** Each further round counted for a marked spot: `new = max(err_mm * cb_error_step_pct / 100, L_now * cb_error_floor_pct / 100)`;
  if `new < err_mm` the offset `at - pos` is scaled by `new / err_mm` (no new draw) and `err_mm = new`. `cb_tick`: while `tick >= decay_at`:
  `evidence -= 1`, `decay_at += cb_decay_s`; at 0 the spot is dropped with its marker (`cb_dropped`). A gun that moved beyond the K1 radius starts a
  new spot; the old marker stays where it was until it decays (D 4.3 displacement). Test `marker_sharpens_to_the_floor` (5 more rounds at 800 m:
  `err_mm` 120 m -> 40 m, the marker moves toward the gun; control step 100: unchanged), `evidence_decays_and_the_marker_goes` (3 rounds, then 900 s:
  gone; control 600 s: still there), `counter_battery_deterministic_threads_and_save_load` (save with a pending and a sharpening marker).
- **K4 Knowledge and the counter-fire flag.** `known_by_record` (`fire_mission.rs` 671) also accepts a point within `intel_snap_m` of one of the
  player's markers, so map fire on a marker is not `UnknownGround`; a marker reveals nothing else (no spotting, no intel cell). In
  `game.rs` `order_gun_mission` (1738) a gun mission whose target is within `intel_snap_m` of an own marker gets `Mission::cb` (`cb_missions`); its
  rounds count `cb_rounds`, its bursts `cb_bursts` and, within `cb_near_m` of a living enemy `inf_gun`, `cb_near_bursts` (`observe_bursts`; bursts
  after the mission ended are not counted: said in the JSON docs). Test `map_fire_on_a_marker_is_accepted` (control `counter_battery` 0:
  `UnknownGround`), `marker_does_not_reveal_the_gun` (the gun's `seen_by` unchanged), `counter_fire_mission_is_flagged` (control: target 60 m off the
  marker: not flagged).

## 6. Determinism, cost, guard rails
One new RNG purpose (`CB_MARK`), drawn once per marker in the sim; none in the AI. All maths `Fx` / integers; spots in a `Vec` in creation order,
players and men by ascending id. Cost: K1 one pass over the hearer's soldiers per gun round (at most 2 guns, 8 rounds a minute); `cb_tick` once a
second over at most 8 spots a player; F2/F3 add own men to lists that are already built per burst, no new ray budget (`MAX_FRAG_RAYS_PER_TICK`
unchanged); S1 is a list walk per own mission per plan, no rays. Tick p99 from `tools/verify.sh` only (bound: not more than 25 % above the S11a1
verify value). No match without a winner; Germany pooled 35-65 %; T8 hard 26.3 %.
