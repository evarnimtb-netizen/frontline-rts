## 0. Why this cut, and what was weighed
The design sizes S11b at 0.4 x S3 "data plus tuning" (D 1477) and warns that it is not "no new code" (D 1705-1707). Read in the code:
- **Field howitzers are data on S11a1's rules.** Every sim rule keys on `VehicleRole::InfGun` (`fire_mission.rs` 869, `gunnery.rs` 848, `supply.rs`
  923, `damage.rs` 1558, `guns.rs` 643 / 929, `ai.rs` 1119 `mix_slot`), so a howitzer with `role = "inf_gun"` takes missions, is heard, rearms
  and is counted with no sim change. New code is only the AI's second buy (`ai/arty.rs` `arty_pot`, `buy_arty` pick the FIRST such roster card).
- **Tows and displacement are cut** (section 1). S9's tow job is AT-gun code (`ai/guns.rs`, 1,501 lines) and needs crates at the gun (S11a2 spec
  12). **Without them a howitzer is a base gun:** it arrives limbered at the base, emplaces there in 90 s, cannot be pushed (D 721-722), re-lays
  in 25 s, and fires flat (D 769-774: leFH 18 max +42 deg, no high angle; the M2A1's high angle starts at 3.3 km, D 750). From the base it hits
  only what its flat arc clears (`mission_refusal` `Masked`), as the M3 does today. That is the design's own verdict for small maps (D 780-797).
- **Light mortars are data** (`Capability::FireMission` squads are driven by `ai/mortar.rs` `mortars` whatever the tube); new code is one switch
  in the buy mix. **HE rifle grenades are the new sim rule** (a second launcher mode beside `combat.rs` `fire_single_shot`).
- **The S11a2 measure's open items come first (A0), as diagnosed** (measured; log top block): T81 is a counting error (X1); counter-fire
  dominates the US gun because nothing makes it alternate (X3); the vehicle buys fell because the gun and observer cards cost manpower (USA
  about 500 MP a match), not through the pot or the shells, so **no key restores them and none is built** (X2 is a finding: S11b-10).
Honest expectation: from the base the howitzers will often be masked; the slice builds and measures them (T150-T154), it does not promise they
matter on Kessel Ridge. A second gun card displaces vehicles further (T158 reports it). The 75 mm pack howitzer is data in no roster (D 157).
