# S15 "USSR": history and figures (working note)

Working note for `S15-ussr.md` (2026-11-26, plan agent). Values here are inputs; the plan decides. The card date is 1944-06, as for
Germany and the USA. Marks: **[read]** = the page was read on 2026-11-26 and the figure is on it; **(verify)** = from general reference
knowledge or one secondary source, not read from a page in this stage: the data stage (Builds B and C) reads a source for each before it
goes into a def's `sources`, and keeps or corrects it. Where two sources disagree, both are given; the points that go to the owner are
listed in section 9.

## 1. Rifle company and squad

| Figure | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Section size (authorised) | **9 men** (shtat 04/550, December 1942, the rifle company in force through 1944) | battleorder.org "USSR rifle company 1944" [read] |

| Heavy section | as above with two DP gunners and two assistants, one PPSh man, two riflemen | same [read] |
| Platoon | 2 heavy and 2 light sections on paper; the fourth was usually left out; 2 snipers (Mosin PU) per platoon | same [read] |
| Field strength | "sections were effectively 6 men" by 1944 (platoons of 4 sections of 6) | same [read] |
| SMG platoon | one per company in 1944: the rifle platoon's frame with every rifle replaced by a PPSh-41 / PPS-43; DP gunners kept; company SMGs 12 -> 51 | same [read] |
| MG section | 6 men: commander, gunner on the Maxim M1910/30 or SG-43, 3 bearers, a cart driver | same [read] |
| Company mortar | 2 x **50 mm RM-38** (7 men), removed by the December 1944 table (in practice early 1945); the 82 mm sits in the battalion mortar company | same [read] |
| AT rifles | not in the company: the battalion's AT rifle platoon | same [read] |
| Medics | a medical instructor (senior sergeant) and 4 medics per company | same [read] |
| Later table | shtat 05/40 (December 1944) replaces 04/550; not read | same [read] (the page names it only) |

Game reading. Strelkovoe otdelenie = the **light section, 9 men** (one DP); the field-strength card = **6 men** (commander PPSh, DP
gunner and assistant, three riflemen). Avtomatchiki = a 9-man section of the SMG platoon with its DP kept. The 50 mm mortar: the design
(`guns-artillery.md` P1, 148) says the 50 mm had left line companies by 1944; battleorder.org keeps two per company until the
December 1944 table. **Sources disagree: owner question S15-6.** The medic team of 2 is a cut of the company's section of 5.

Game reading. Plate: `mm = 2`, front torso only. Against the game's weapons this means: pistol-calibre rounds (MP 40, P 38, M3, M1911,
Thompson-class) and light fragments stop; every rifle and MG round goes through at any range (Kar98k 7 mm at 0 m, 3 mm at 300 m in
`kar98k.toml`). ROKS-3 on the FmW 41 pattern: 10 L over about 8 shots of 1 s at 12 particles a second = 96 units of about 0.1 L,
`range_m` 35 (between the read 45 m maximum and the 15-25 m effective figure): **two fuel figures (9 L and 10 L): the plan takes 10 L and
marks it; a data point, not an owner question** (the difference is one shot).

## 7. Doctrine notes the plan uses

- Fire direction: Soviet artillery calls were slower and heavier (`artillery-on-map.md` 4.8 wants `fire_direction_s` per faction: USA 15,
  Germany 25, USSR 40). The key is one rules value today (`tuning.rs` 2351): not built in S15, listed for the balance pass.
- Radios were scarce at company level (`infantry-structures.md` 2.3): the observer team keeps the radio (the game's radio link is the only
  way a gun mission is called).
- Many Soviet medics were women (`infantry-structures.md` 154): a presenter note for the voice and model pass, not a rule.
- Penal units are not shipped (`infantry-structures.md` 2.3).
