## 1. Scope
**In.** (A) Sim: a served gun whose main weapon has an `[indirect]` table takes `FireMission`; low- and high-angle solutions with a mask check; gun
aim-error keys; the radio link; one rearm switch. (B) Content and measures: leIG 18, 105 mm M3, VB-Trupp, FO Party, two HE rounds; four fortify rows;
JSON block `s11a1`. (C) AI: buy one gun and one observer team, emplace in base, an observation post, observed missions, a shell budget, lifting,

and AI crate carrying (a crate does not refill a gun today: new sim rule and new AI; S11a1-2). Friendly fire Q22 (changes every mortar mission of both

base, D 4.9; S11a1-4). HEAT rounds (would move the armour rows T31/T54 in the same measure; S11a1-8). Holding a whole assault for `prep_fire_s`
(changes every attack; S11a1-6). Per-faction fire-direction delay, `range_pe_pct` /
`deflection_pe_mrad`, crater scaling by filler, `howitzer_pit`, the load price check, the in-flight guard, the artillery cap counting neutral guns,
precomputed observation posts, the HQ radio relay, radio handover.
**Later (presenter pass, not S11a2 sim work).** Gun and FO models, the fire-mission tool on a gun, the targeting overlay, refusal words, incoming
whistle, the Q24 hint. Until then a human can buy the cards but has no gun mission tool (S11a1-9).
